# Passive firing resources

`resources.c` is the bounded C energy and heat kernel for the DOS firing trainer. `resources.h` exposes `ResourceProfile`, `ResourceState`, `resource_load`, `resource_reset`, `resource_tick`, `resource_can_fire`, and `resource_fire`. `assets.py::stage(out)` copies the native getter-derived `RESOURCE.DAT`; call `python3 dos/resources/check.py` to regenerate and qualify it. Docker is used for native linking, sanitizer checks, DOS compilation and DOSBox execution.

The stock profile is a loaded Sparrow with its original outfits and one training Energy Blaster as the firing cost. The stock Sparrow carries Beam Lasers; the Energy Blaster replaces their costs only for this trainer. Native getters yield 4000 battery energy, 1.9 energy per tick, 2.9 passive heat per tick, .00077 heat dissipation, 18600 maximum heat, and per-shot 5.8 energy and 18 heat. The profile is staged as `ESRESOURCE1` plus seven decimal values in that order. `resource_load` rejects missing, malformed, nonfinite or out-of-range values, including negative capacities and shot costs; signed net generation is allowed; staging checks native source, ship, weapon and outfit data hashes.

A tick clamps energy to capacity *before* generation, dissipates heat, sets overheat only when heat is strictly above maximum, clears it only when heat is strictly below 90% maximum, and generates energy and passive heat only while active. It then clamps both levels nonnegative. This follows `Ship::DoGeneration`. `resource_can_fire` combines the Ship action gate for overheating with `Ship::CanFire`'s resource check; `resource_fire` uses the effect of `Ship::ExpendAmmo` for the Energy Blaster. A shot may push heat above maximum; the overheat flag changes on the following tick. Overheat blocks gun firing in the trainer. Flight engines remain fully supplied as an explicit trainer departure.

The oracle links unchanged upstream native objects and directly calls `Ship::DoGeneration`, `Ship::CanFire`, and `Ship::ExpendAmmo` on copies of the loaded Sparrow. A test-only header visibility macro exposes the private methods and state to the oracle; no upstream source is changed. Nine scenarios cover sustained stock firing, burst, energy starvation and release, heat overrun and recovery, exact heat thresholds, and energy overflow. Synthetic profiles change only capacity, generation or heat capacity and zero passive heat generation in the heat stress cases. Native ASan/UBSan and DOSBox at 16 MiB / fixed 20,000 cycles compare all 927 trace rows; the recorded result is in `dos/reports/resource-equivalence.json`.

This slice excludes movement energy/heat, hull and shield repair, status effects, fuel, solar collection, active cooling, ship damage and actual hardpoint cooldown. The oracle uses full-health ships so repair is idle. The trainer supplies movement independently. No claim is made about full-game energy behavior.
